Bake Ratio — Privacy

Last changed: November 2026

The short version

Bake Ratio has no networking code. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go.

What the app stores

Saved recipes, the bake log and your display settings are written to a single file inside the app's own container on your iPhone. They are not synced, not backed up to any service we run, and not shared with anyone.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising networks
  • No third-party libraries that could observe what you do
  • No access to camera, photos, contacts, location, health data or notifications
  • No tracking authorisation request (there is nothing to track)

Removing your data

Delete the app. That removes the stored file, the saved recipes and the bake log permanently. There is no copy anywhere else.

Children

The app is a kitchen calculator. It is not aimed at children and it collects no data from anyone of any age.

If this changes

Any future version that stores something new will describe it here first, with a new date, and will still require no account.
