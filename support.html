How the calculator works

Every percentage is measured against the total flour weight — that is the usual convention in bread baking. Hydration 75% means the water weighs 75% of the flour. Salt and starter are calculated the same way.

The starter is treated as a 100% hydration starter: half flour, half water. The app shows that split under the ingredient list, and the result card adds it to the true totals so you can see the real flour and water in the dough.

Common questions

Why does the total dough weigh more than flour + water?

Because salt and starter are included, and the starter itself carries water and flour. The result card breaks this down: total dough, weight per loaf, true total flour, true total water.

Can I start from the weight of the finished dough instead?

Yes. Switch the segmented control at the top to "Start from dough weight" and the app works backwards to the flour weight needed.

How accurate is the fermentation estimate?

It is a rough window, not an instruction. It starts from a room-temperature and starter-amount model, then widens the range. Flour strength, starter vigour, dough temperature and shaping all move it. Judge the dough: look for a domed top, bubbles at the edge and a 30–50% rise.

Where are my recipes stored?

On this device, in a single file inside the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account and no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes the saved recipes and bake log.

Can I export my recipes?

There is no export in this version. If that matters to you, email us — it is the most requested feature.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The app contains no networking code at all.

Does it tell me when the bread is ready?

No. The timer is a countdown for your own bulk ferment; it is not a probe and it cannot see your dough. It never sends notifications — the timer runs only while the screen is open.
